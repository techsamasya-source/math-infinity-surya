Privacy Policy

Table Practice is a maths learning tool for practising multiplication tables, squares, square roots, cubes and cube roots.

Information stored on your device

This version may store answers, practice history, points and preferences in your browser's local storage. This information remains in the browser/device and is not automatically synced to the developer or to other devices. Clearing browser data or changing devices may remove this information.

Accounts and third-party services

This version does not require an account for core practice and does not intentionally send your local practice history to the developer. If advertising, analytics, cloud accounts, payments or other third-party services are added later, this policy must be updated to describe the actual data collected, used and shared by those services.

Usage analytics

Usage analytics are not active until the developer configures a Google Analytics 4 Measurement ID in the app. If enabled, events such as selected practice mode, selected table, dashboard views and report views may be collected to understand aggregate feature usage. Do not enter personal or sensitive information into analytics events.

Children's privacy

Parents or guardians should supervise children's use as appropriate. Before public release, the developer must confirm the app's target audience and comply with applicable child-safety, advertising and privacy requirements.

Data choices

Practice history can be removed using the app's reset controls or by clearing the browser's site data. Exported progress files, if created by the user, remain under the user's control.
